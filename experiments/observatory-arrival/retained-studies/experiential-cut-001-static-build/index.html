01THE OBSERVER

Field observer · unfinished listener

Meet Zee.

Study how humans receive one another’s realities, record where information travels, and remain curious when certainty feels more comfortable.

Zee did not come to correct humans. Zee came because human interaction produces something the instruments cannot fully explain.

A fragment from Zee’s world

Feelings rarely remain entirely inside the beings who feel them.

They gather between participants like weather. Grief lowers the ceiling. Urgency moves like wind. Embarrassment creates a warm violet fog everyone politely pretends is atmospheric.

Earth is different. Humans carry entire climates beneath the skin.

“Zee initially considered this a design defect. Mara called it privacy.”

03FIELD INSTRUMENTS

Tools for noticing what words leave invisible.

The instruments do not measure goodness, intelligence, or truth. They help Zee remain curious about what a conversation permits next.

◉INSTRUMENT 01

Receptivity Meter

≋INSTRUMENT 02

◒INSTRUMENT 03

Burden Scale

Reveals who receives the next assignment to explain, prove, remember, or adapt.

⌁INSTRUMENT 04

Field Notebook

Keeps questions open long enough for reality to become larger than the first conclusion.
