# Image prompts — A Field Guide for an Alien Learning to Listen

These prompts establish one consistent visual world for the interactive page, shorts, and future book. Generate in a **vertical 9:16 crop for shorts** and a **wide 16:9 crop for the website**. Keep the same character references across every image.

## Shared art direction

Gentle cinematic editorial illustration for thoughtful young readers aged 13–16; quiet science-fiction realism mixed with hand-painted storybook textures; deep midnight indigo, dusty violet, soft cyan and small warm amber accents; emotionally precise body language; subtle humor; soft directional lighting; restrained detail; inclusive contemporary school setting; no text, logos, watermarks, exaggerated cartoon comedy, horror, weapons, or threatening alien imagery.

Zee is a small, non-threatening alien observer with a slightly elongated oval head, muted violet-blue skin, large luminous pale-cyan eyes, a compact midnight field suit, a small cross-body field notebook, and an earnest curious posture. Zee should appear observant and socially awkward rather than cute, powerful, or superior.

Mara is a 15-year-old human student with dark wavy shoulder-length hair, thoughtful brown eyes, a practical rust-colored cardigan over a navy school outfit, and a worn canvas bag. She is intelligent and perceptive but not presented as perfectly confident or morally flawless.

## 1. Hero — Zee arrives to study listening

A wide atmospheric portrait of Zee standing quietly inside a dim school corridor after hours, holding an open field notebook, classroom doors and stacked chairs receding behind them like an unfamiliar human landscape, a tiny receptivity meter glowing soft violet at their belt, curious rather than dramatic mood, generous dark negative space around Zee for website title placement, cinematic editorial storybook illustration, shared art direction.

Suggested file: `images/01-zee-arrival.webp`

## 2. Everyone heard Mara

A student council meeting around a rectangular classroom table, Mara has just finished speaking and the other students are nodding politely while already turning their attention toward a projected plan at the front, the adult chairperson is kind-faced and hurried rather than villainous, Zee watches from a slightly open doorway with puzzled concentration, visual contradiction between apparent attention and unchanged momentum, shared art direction.

Suggested file: `images/02-everyone-heard-mara.webp`

## 3. The receptivity meter

Close-up of Zee’s small handheld alien instrument scanning a human meeting, elegant semicircular interface glowing from sealed amber through uncertain violet to open green, reflections of seated students visible in its glass, tactile invented technology, no readable words or interface text, macro cinematic illustration, shared art direction.

Suggested file: `images/03-receptivity-meter.webp`

## 4. The room pauses

The same student council room a moment later: one quiet student has gently raised a hand and everyone has paused before continuing, Mara looks surprised but not triumphant, someone is erasing one small part of the plan on the board, Zee watches the receptivity meter shift toward green, the repair is modest and believable, the room has not become magically harmonious, shared art direction.

Suggested file: `images/04-the-room-pauses.webp`

## 5. Seven pebbles

Zee kneeling on a school courtyard path, arranging seven ordinary pebbles in a line while several humans individually inspect stones with magnifying glasses and fail to notice the path the stones form, Mara stands nearby beginning to recognize the pattern, subtle visual humor, metaphorical but grounded, shared art direction.

Suggested file: `images/05-seven-pebbles.webp`

## 6. The joke with no owner

A group of teenagers after a joke has landed badly; several students hold up apologetic empty hands as if no one owns what happened, one student sits damp beneath a tiny impossible rain cloud while everyone else remains dry, Zee studies the cloud and takes a field note, compassionate focus on the affected student, gently surreal visual metaphor, shared art direction.

Suggested file: `images/06-joke-no-owner.webp`

## 7. The boundary debate society

Mara places a simple glowing boundary line around a private phone conversation while another student stands outside it holding a long scroll of reasonable-sounding explanations, Zee examines the line as information rather than a wall, modern bedroom and group-chat atmosphere blended through subtle visual metaphor, no readable phone text, shared art direction.

Suggested file: `images/07-boundary-debate.webp`

## 8. Closing image — unfinished listeners

Zee and Mara walking together at dusk through a school courtyard beneath a subtle star-filled sky, carrying the field notebook between them, behind them an imperfect circle of students moves one chair to make room for someone arriving, hopeful but unresolved, listening represented as a door of soft light being reopened rather than a final victory, wide composition with negative space for a closing question, shared art direction.

Suggested file: `images/08-unfinished-listeners.webp`

## Practical consistency note

Generate the hero reference first. Select one final design for Zee and Mara, then use that image as the visual reference for all later generations. Avoid changing their clothes, age, proportions, or color palette between scenes.
