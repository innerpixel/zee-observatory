# Historical Arrival development

This folder preserves the exploratory path that preceded the current Arrival
continuation set.

The documents here include early landing concepts, premise changes, the broad
Arrival vision, its clarification exchange, and deferred station exploration.
They are evidence and source history, not current specifications.

The subfolder `realistic-orbital-arrival/` preserves the later branch in which
Arrival was developed as a realistic vessel-and-station encounter near Earth.
Its interaction discoveries remain influential, but that visual and narrative
frame has been superseded by the active TAIC Territory direction.

For current work, begin with `../../CONTINUATION.md`.
