# Evening Fuel — Fruits of Nature

*A mycelial solar-melon elixir that goes directly to the soul.*

## Ingredients

- the seeded center of a yellow melon;
- a few pieces of the melon;
- turmeric;
- black pepper;
- cayenne pepper;
- bee pollen;
- bee bread;
- MCT oil;
- a little almond oil;
- honey;
- a little salt;
- a little turkey tail tincture.

The turkey tail tincture is the source of the small amount of alcohol.

## Method

Place everything in a blender.

Run it through the blender **five times**, until thoroughly transformed.

Serve cold.
