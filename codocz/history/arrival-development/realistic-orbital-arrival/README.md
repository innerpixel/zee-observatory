# Realistic orbital Arrival branch

This folder preserves the conceptual and interaction studies developed while
Arrival was framed as a vessel near a realistic Observatory in Earth orbit.

That direction produced durable discoveries:

- the event must exist independently of the visitor;
- attention may increase local resolution without causing the event;
- the canvas must create a question before interface language resolves it;
- reading time and perceptual time are different;
- recurring observation can teach behaviour without a tutorial;
- no action is required and return to quiet is not failure;
- the interface may report evidence but must not diagnose identity.

Those principles have been restated in the active TAIC Territory direction.
The documents here remain valuable reasoning history, but their Earth-orbit,
realistic-station, carrier-cut, layered-station, and rail proposals are not the
current visual or experiential starting point.

The snapshot
`observatory.current-project-brief.before-taic-territory.md` records the handoff
exactly as it stood before the pivot.

For current work, begin with `../../../CONTINUATION.md`.
