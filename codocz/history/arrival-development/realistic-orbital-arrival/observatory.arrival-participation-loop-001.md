# Arrival Participation Loop 001

*One carrier reaches local Observatory reception. The world continues.*

## Purpose

This is a paper prototype for one complete exterior encounter. It tests whether
the Arrival can embody the wider CSMCL.SPACE relationship without explaining
the wider architecture.

The event must remain complete when the Traveller:

```text
notices only
does nothing
listens deliberately
inspects the Observatory instead
requests approach independently
leaves at any point
```

No path is scored. Listening is deeper attention, not success. The carrier is
not a disguised entrance button, and the Observatory does not close when it is
ignored.

---

## Central invariant

```text
The carrier reaches Observatory reception because it was already travelling
through the field—not because the Traveller noticed it.
```

Attention changes local legibility. It does not cause the carrier, complete its
journey, or make the world wait.

---

## Event proposition

A small vessel emits an open, unaddressed carrier. The carrier crosses the
local field and reaches a specific exterior receiving node on the Observatory.
The node changes state and emits one narrowly scoped receipt pulse back along
the carrier relation.

The Traveller's local receiver can detect parts of this event. Deliberate
tuning stabilizes one incomplete carrier fragment. It does not send a reply,
identify the Traveller, or alter Observatory reception.

Later, the same carrier pattern recurs from a changed position. This supports
recognition while complicating any first assumption that the vessel was simply
lost, waiting, approaching, or leaving.

---

## Causal ownership

| Participant | What it does | What it does not do |
|---|---|---|
| Carrier source | Emits an open carrier continuously; changes position later | Does not address the Traveller, request docking, or request a reply |
| Observatory receiving node | Receives the carrier and emits a receipt pulse scoped to that carrier relation | Does not greet, identify, enroll, or interpret the Traveller |
| Traveller-local receiver | Detects observable carrier and receiver conditions; may stabilize a fragment when tuned | Does not know motive, identity, destination, or emotional state |
| Traveller attention | Makes one local fragment more legible and holds it longer | Does not cause reception, prove preference, or create a profile |
| Ambient world | Continues traffic, orbit, and unrelated activity throughout | Does not pause while the Traveller reads |

### Does any source notice the Traveller?

No.

In Loop 001, the carrier source and Observatory receiving node do not detect or
respond to the Traveller. The local interface notices only local interaction
with its own controls. Observatory approach remains a separate available
relation governed by the existing arrival behavior.

---

## Two clocks

The loop distinguishes:

```text
WORLD CLOCK
  carrier motion · reception · receipt pulse · recurrence
  continues independently

INTERFACE CLOCK
  source resolution · observation · facts · optional controls
  respects minimum perception time and may pause during attention
```

If a world event occurs while the Traveller holds a packet, the visual event
still happens. Its interface description waits rather than replacing what the
Traveller is reading.

This prevents attention from stopping the world while also preventing the
interface from racing past perception.

## Event score

Timing values are initial perception hypotheses, not production constants.

### Beat 1 — PRE-SIGNAL :: a relation begins becoming perceptible

**World event**

The existing vessel continues emitting. A faint, discontinuous pulse becomes
visible along part of the space between vessel and Observatory.

**Visible or audible change**

One moving point appears intermittently on a previously quiet trajectory. It
does not yet reach either endpoint. No audio is required.

**Source of information**

World layer only. The local interface has not resolved a source.

**Observation sentence**

None yet.

**Field · Relation · Agency packet**

None yet. Showing telemetry before the event can be perceived would explain
before allowing anticipation.

**Minimum perception time**

6–9 seconds after the first visible pulse.

**Condition for progression**

World clock. The next pulse advances far enough to establish a trajectory.

**Passive continuation**

The pulse continues whether or not it is noticed.

**Optional Traveller response**

Existing Observatory inspection and approach controls remain independently
available. No carrier control appears yet.

**Aftereffect**

None.

**What remains unresolved**

Whether this is a route, reflection, transmission, moving object, or decorative
field behavior.

**What may recur later**

Pulse cadence and mineral-violet color.

**Wholeness check**

Nothing suggests the event exists to diagnose, summon, or complete the visitor.

**Playability check**

Noticing is already participation; no prompt converts noticing into a task.

**Accessibility / reduced-motion equivalent**

A low-motion point appears sequentially at three fixed positions rather than
travelling smoothly. It is not conveyed through color alone: faint intensity
and position also change.

---

### Beat 2 — WORLD EVENT :: the carrier enters local reception

**World event**

The pulse trajectory becomes continuous enough to connect the vessel's carrier
with one Observatory receiving node.

**Visible or audible change**

The moving point completes the final segment to the node. The node remains in
its prior state for a short interval; reception is not yet declared.

**Source of information**

World layer, followed by Traveller-local receiver detection.

**Observation sentence**

After the visual relation has existed long enough to notice:

> A carrier has entered local reception.

**Field · Relation · Agency packet**

```text
FIELD      carrier present
RELATION   receiving node intersected
AGENCY     reply requested: no
```

“Intersected” is provisional interface language. It means the detected carrier
path now meets the node; it does not yet claim successful receipt.

**Minimum perception time**

12–16 seconds for the observation and packet together.

**Condition for progression**

World clock continues toward node state change. Interface clock does not
replace this packet before its minimum duration.

**Passive continuation**

The carrier proceeds into the receiving condition.

**Optional Traveller response**

`Attend to carrier` becomes available. `Inspect Observatory` remains equally
available. `Remain` requires no control activation.

**Aftereffect**

The receiving node is now causally prepared to change state.

**What remains unresolved**

Carrier content, source identity, intended destination, duration, reason, and
whether the node will accept or reject it.

**What may recur later**

Carrier pattern and source-to-node relation.

**Wholeness check**

No language implies that the carrier or visitor lacks something the platform
must supply.

**Playability check**

The Traveller may investigate either participant or remain with the whole
field. There is no correct object.

**Accessibility / reduced-motion equivalent**

The source-to-node relation gains a stable patterned line and the rail reports
the event. A screen-reader live announcement occurs once after the visual
anticipation interval, not repeatedly for each pulse.

---

### Beat 3 — ANTICIPATION :: reception has not yet been interpreted

**World event**

The carrier remains present at the receiving node. Ambient traffic continues
through unrelated trajectories.

**Visible or audible change**

The endpoint holds. The station does not immediately flash, speak, or explain.

**Source of information**

World layer. Existing local packet remains available.

**Observation sentence**

No new sentence.

**Field · Relation · Agency packet**

The Beat 2 packet remains. Facts are not replaced merely because time passes.

**Minimum perception time**

8–12 seconds of held relation before the receiving node changes.

**Condition for progression**

World clock only. Tuning does not accelerate or delay reception.

**Passive continuation**

The Traveller has time to look between source, trajectory, and node.

**Optional Traveller response**

Attend to the carrier, inspect the Observatory, request approach independently,
remain, or leave.

**Aftereffect**

None yet.

**What remains unresolved**

Whether reception is occurring, whether the carrier has a destination, and
whether any response will follow.

**What may recur later**

The held endpoint relation may become recognizable in another receipt event.

**Wholeness check**

The interface tolerates uncertainty instead of using it to pressure action.

**Playability check**

The interval creates room to anticipate and compare, not a countdown.

**Accessibility / reduced-motion equivalent**

No additional motion is required. The stable relation and retained packet
carry the beat.

---

### Beat 4 — OPTIONAL TUNING :: attention stabilizes one fragment

This beat may occur before, during, or after the receiving aftereffect. It is an
interface branch, not a branch in the world's causality.

**World event**

No new world event is caused by tuning. Carrier and Observatory timelines
continue.

**Visible or audible change**

The local carrier trace becomes less intermittent. Ambient sound, if later
used, lowers rather than making the carrier louder by default.

**Source of information**

Traveller-local receiver isolates a fragment of the carrier.

**Observation sentence**

> One fragment remains stable while attended.

**Field · Relation · Agency packet**

```text
FIELD      fragment locally stable
RELATION   source unchanged
AGENCY     reply path not opened
```

**Incomplete carrier fragment — candidate, not canon**

```text
…reception is sufficient…
```

The ellipses are meaningful: the local receiver has not recovered the complete
statement.

**Minimum perception time**

The tuned packet remains until the Traveller releases it or leaves. It is not
automatically replaced by a timer.

**Condition for progression**

World progression continues independently. Interface progression queues later
descriptions while the tuned packet is held.

**Passive continuation**

If the Traveller does not tune, no carrier words appear. The world event still
remains complete through its visible reception and aftereffect.

**Optional Traveller response**

Release frequency, inspect source, inspect Observatory, remain, or use the
independent approach relation. No reply control exists in Loop 001.

**Aftereffect**

Local legibility only. No remote source changes because of attention.

**What remains unresolved**

The missing words, intended listener, meaning of “sufficient,” source identity,
and whether the statement is current or repeated.

**What may recur later**

The fragment may return with one additional word or a changed source position,
never as a reward for having tuned previously.

**Wholeness check**

Listening is not framed as rescue, intimacy, or proof of the Traveller's nature.

**Playability check**

Attention changes resolution and duration, not score or access entitlement.

**Accessibility / reduced-motion equivalent**

Tuning is available through a labelled keyboard control. The stable fragment is
presented as text; audio is supplementary. Focus does not time out.

---

### Beat 5 — AFTEREFFECT :: the Observatory records receipt

**World event**

The receiving node changes from amber to mineral green and emits one short
receipt pulse back along the carrier relation.

This is specifically:

```text
the Observatory receiving node acknowledging receipt of this carrier
```

It is not the Observatory acknowledging the Traveller, and it is not a reply
from the Traveller.

**Visible or audible change**

Node color and pulse cadence change. One return pulse travels toward the source.
The wider station does not illuminate or become active as a reward.

**Source of information**

World layer first; Traveller-local receiver reports only after direct visual
evidence exists.

**Observation sentence**

> The receiving node has returned a receipt pulse.

**Field · Relation · Agency packet**

```text
FIELD      receipt pulse emitted
RELATION   carrier source targeted
AGENCY     Traveller reply: not requested
```

“Targeted” describes the pulse trajectory back toward the detected source. It
does not imply identity knowledge.

**Minimum perception time**

16–22 seconds. If a tuned packet is held, this description waits in the local
interface while the visible event still occurs.

**Condition for progression**

The receipt pulse completes its visible path. The node then slowly returns to
its quiet receiving state.

**Passive continuation**

This beat happens whether the Traveller tuned, inspected, remained, or was
reading another packet.

**Optional Traveller response**

Inspect the changed node, attend to the carrier if still locally available,
remain, approach independently, or leave.

**Aftereffect**

The carrier-node relation now has observable evidence of receipt.

**What remains unresolved**

Whether the source receives or understands the pulse, what the Observatory does
with the carrier, and whether this is routine or exceptional.

**What may recur later**

The node state, receipt-pulse form, and carrier recurrence key.

**Wholeness check**

Receipt is a world relation, not a promise of belonging or personal recognition.

**Playability check**

The visible consequence rewards attention with understanding, not points or
exclusive content. It remains visible to passive observers.

**Accessibility / reduced-motion equivalent**

The node changes shape or halo pattern as well as color. The return path may
resolve as a static segmented trace. The rail states who emitted what and toward
which relation.

---

### Beat 6 — ECHO :: the first interpretation becomes less stable

**World event**

After an interval of unrelated traffic and quiet, the same carrier pattern
reappears from a slightly changed source position.

**Visible or audible change**

The recurrence begins from a displaced point. It does not immediately complete
another full receipt cycle.

**Source of information**

Traveller-local receiver compares carrier pattern, not Traveller behavior.

**Observation sentence**

> The same carrier pattern has returned from elsewhere in the field.

**Field · Relation · Agency packet**

```text
FIELD      carrier pattern recurring
RELATION   source position changed
AGENCY     follow available · commitment none
```

“Follow” means hold attention on the local carrier trace. It does not move the
Traveller, subscribe them, or preserve a preference.

**Minimum perception time**

16–22 seconds, then held until passive return is chosen or the carrier leaves
local listening range.

**Condition for progression**

Carrier passes beyond local resolution or the Traveller returns to passive
field. No narrative completion is declared.

**Passive continuation**

The recurrence appears briefly and leaves. Missing it creates no loss; the
relation may recur in another session or surface if later designed.

**Optional Traveller response**

Follow locally, inspect the vessel, inspect Observatory, remain, or leave.

**Aftereffect**

The first interpretation—lost, waiting, approaching, departing—has additional
but still insufficient evidence.

**What remains unresolved**

Reason for movement, destination, identity, and whether the recurrence is
intentional.

**What may recur later**

The source identity, carrier pattern, receipt relation, and incomplete fragment
may support recognition at the station or another exterior visit.

**Wholeness check**

Recurrence belongs to the world event. It is not evidence that the Traveller
needs, prefers, or identifies with this source.

**Playability check**

The Traveller can revise an interpretation without being told they were wrong.

**Accessibility / reduced-motion equivalent**

The source marker resolves at a new labelled spatial position. The interface
states recurrence and displacement without relying on animated comparison.

---

### Beat 7 — RETURN TO QUIET :: the event remains unfinished but complete

**World event**

The carrier leaves local resolution. The receiving node returns to its quiet
available state. Ambient traffic continues.

**Visible or audible change**

Carrier trace fades; node halo settles; unrelated motion remains.

**Source of information**

World layer. The local receiver returns to passive field after preserving the
last packet for its minimum readable duration.

**Observation sentence**

> Local reception has returned to quiet.

This sentence may be omitted if the visual change is sufficiently clear.

**Field · Relation · Agency packet**

```text
FIELD      local carrier unresolved
RELATION   receiving node available
AGENCY     action required: none
```

**Minimum perception time**

No forced replacement. Quiet continues indefinitely.

**Condition for progression**

None. The loop is complete without a completion state.

**Passive continuation**

The Observatory remains approachable. Other world processes may occur later.

**Optional Traveller response**

Remain, inspect Observatory, request approach, revisit a still-available local
fact, use the existing field archive, or leave.

**Aftereffect**

No reward, badge, unlocked destination, or retained personal preference.

**What remains unresolved**

The carrier's identity, purpose, destination, complete content, and later
relation to the Observatory.

**What may recur later**

The world may recognize the carrier-event relation later without claiming that
the Traveller formed a relationship with it.

**Wholeness check**

Nothing about the Traveller becomes incomplete if they do not continue.

**Playability check**

The event offered noticing, comparison, tuning, revision, and departure without
turning any of them into progression requirements.

**Accessibility / reduced-motion equivalent**

State is communicated through stable text and node pattern. No disappearance
occurs before the minimum reading duration; focusable content remains available
until focus moves.

## Stopping-point completeness

| Traveller stops after | What they still experienced | What they did not lose |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-signal | A living field containing unexplained motion | No required clue or route |
| Carrier relation | A signal crossed toward a station node | No obligation to identify or listen |
| Anticipation | The world tolerated uncertainty | No countdown or failed task |
| Tuning | One fragment became locally clearer | No exclusive progression entitlement |
| Receipt aftereffect | The Observatory visibly received something | No requirement to follow its source |
| Echo | Earlier meaning became revisable | No demand to solve the carrier |
| Quiet | The place remained whole after the event | No artificial loss for leaving |

## Carrier and Observatory independence

The current Observatory controls remain available throughout the loop.

```text
carrier relation
  listen · follow locally · release

Observatory relation
  inspect · request approach · remain outside
```

Neither control group unlocks the other. If a later story reveals a relation
between carrier and Observatory, it emerges through world evidence, not route
gating.

---

## Explicit non-effects of attention

Tuning does not:

```text
make the carrier reach the station
cause the receiving node to change
send a presence pulse
open a reply path
identify the Traveller
record a lasting preference
import activity into Attunement
make the carrier recur
```

Tuning may:

```text
stabilize one local fragment
slow or hold the local packet
make a carrier trace more legible
offer a local follow action while the source remains present
```

## Interpretation test

### 1. What can a fresh visitor directly know?

A fresh visitor can directly know only that:

```text
a small source exists in the exterior field
a repeated carrier pattern crosses toward a station node
the carrier reaches that node
the node changes state afterward
the node emits a pulse back along the source relation
the same carrier pattern later appears from a changed position
no reply or identity exchange is requested from the Traveller
the Observatory remains independently inspectable and approachable
```

If tuned, the visitor may also directly know that the local receiver stabilized
the incomplete fragment `…reception is sufficient…`.

They cannot directly know a public source name, motive, destination, emotion,
intended audience, or complete message.

### 2. What may they reasonably infer?

Reasonable provisional inferences include:

- the Observatory node is designed to receive some kind of carrier;
- the return pulse is related to that receipt;
- the carrier source is moving or the observed source position changed;
- reception may matter without docking;
- the signal may have existed before this visit;
- the event would likely have occurred without the visitor.

These are invitations to interpretation, not facts the interface should state
as verdicts.

### 3. What remains deliberately unresolved?

```text
source identity
whether the source is Zee
carrier purpose
complete content
intended destination or listener
why docking was not requested
whether the source receives or understands the receipt pulse
whether the carrier is searching, waiting, departing, lost, or observing
what the Observatory does with received material
whether the relation will recur elsewhere
```

### 4. What changes if they do nothing?

The world changes:

```text
carrier becomes perceptible
carrier reaches reception
node changes state
receipt pulse returns
carrier later recurs from a changed position
field returns to quiet
```

The Traveller is not changed by the system. No profile, preference, identity,
commitment, or retained history is created.

The incomplete carrier words do not become locally stabilized, but the visible
event remains coherent without them.

### 5. What changes if they tune toward the carrier?

Only local legibility changes:

```text
carrier trace stabilizes
one incomplete fragment becomes readable / optionally audible
the current packet remains available longer
a local follow action may exist while the carrier remains present
```

Reception, node state, return pulse, recurrence, and Observatory approach do not
change because of tuning.

### 6. What future recognition could this event support without naming that future now?

The world may later recognize:

```text
this carrier pattern has appeared before
this receiving node previously registered this carrier relation
the source position or route has changed
an incomplete fragment now has additional evidence
```

This can support station-side recognition, an exterior recurrence, or a later
relation view without telling the Arrival visitor about station archives,
Firmament, Innerstellar, or Nexus.

It does not support “you cared about this before” without a separate explicit
personal retention crossing.

### 7. Where could the design accidentally become an attention funnel?

Risks include:

- making tuning necessary to trigger the receipt aftereffect;
- withholding the coherent event unless the Traveller clicks;
- turning the moving pulse into a countdown;
- making the carrier unlock Observatory approach;
- making recurrence conditional on prior attention;
- using increasingly urgent rail messages to prevent departure;
- presenting frequency tuning as a collection or completion mechanic;
- making the incomplete fragment sound personally addressed;
- allowing passive story to become a continuous subtitle feed.

The design avoids these by keeping world causality independent, limiting the
loop to one event, returning to quiet, and leaving all exits open.

### 8. Where could the interface falsely imply that the system understands the Traveller?

False implication would arise if it said or enacted:

```text
we noticed your interest
the Observatory responded to your attention
you chose this carrier
this signal is meant for you
your silence has been recorded
welcome back to this frequency
based on what you explored…
```

It would also arise if node state changed only after tuning, if a frequency
remained selected across sessions without consent, or if the rail personalized
agency language from one action.

The local interface may report `attention mode: tuned` during the current
interaction. It may not translate that transient condition into personal truth.

### 9. What is the smallest reversible implementation slice?

Keep `/arrival/`, the current temporary exterior plate, existing route safety,
and current Observatory controls. Add only:

```text
one separately authored Loop 001 data file
one carrier world timeline with bounded causal phases
one independent interface packet timeline
one moving source pulse ending at one receiving node
one node state change and scoped receipt pulse
one optional local tuning state
one incomplete fragment
one recurrence echo
one return to indefinite quiet
reduced-motion and keyboard equivalents
no persistence
```

Do not build multiple feeds, a channel library, universal rules engine, station
archive, message history, analytics, or cross-session continuity.

The data file may initially be an imported typed content module so content is
separate from route rendering without introducing a generalized runtime feed.
Moving it to runtime-fetched JSON remains an explicit later decision after one
event proves the schema.

### 10. Which questions require human agreement before coding?

All questions in **Explicit open decisions** below.


## Explicit open decisions

These require human agreement before production code:

1. **Carrier fragment** — Is `…reception is sufficient…` the correct first
   fragment, or does it close interpretation too strongly?
2. **Receipt behavior** — Does an Observatory receiving node canonically emit a
   pulse back toward the source after receipt?
3. **Node location** — Which visible structure in the current plate is the
   specific receiving node, and can it remain spatially stable responsively?
4. **Source location** — Which vessel/object is the carrier source in Loop 001?
5. **Local interface ownership** — Is the rail the Traveller's local receiver,
   a shared field convention, or intentionally unnamed at Arrival?
6. **Public lens names** — Are `Field · Relation · Agency` visible headings or
   internal authoring roles only?
7. **Tuning verb** — Use `Attend`, `Listen`, `Tune`, or another term?
8. **Tuned duration** — Does the packet remain until explicit release, carrier
   departure, or either condition?
9. **Passive timing** — How long should anticipation and quiet last before the
   event becomes legible?
10. **Echo timing** — Must recurrence happen in the same visit, or may Loop 001
    end after receipt while recurrence remains future-bearing?
11. **Return pulse visibility** — Is a full moving return pulse too explanatory,
    or is node-state change alone too causally vague?
12. **Approach coexistence** — Should the current approach controls remain
    continuously visible during tuned attention or become locally quieter
    without being disabled?
13. **Content file boundary** — Begin with an imported typed event module or a
    runtime-fetched JSON file?
14. **Accessibility wording** — What is the clearest non-visual description of
    source, trajectory, node change, and recurrence without adding certainty?
15. **Session retention** — Confirm that all attention state is discarded on
    exit and that only authored world relations persist in project content.

Until these are settled, the event score remains a paper prototype.
