# Arrival :: Experiential Cut 001

*One unknown transmission reaches one receiving point. The field continues.*

The visitor may leave during any moment with a complete encounter. Attention
changes only what their local receiver can resolve; it does not cause the
transmission, reception, or aftereffect.

## Variant A — quiet

The canvas carries almost everything. The rail confirms only conditions that
cannot be mistaken for interpretation.

| Moment | Canvas change | Possible question forming | Rail contribution | Minimum pause | Remains unresolved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quiet | Observatory, vessel, Earth, and distant movement remain present | What kind of place is this? | Silence | Indefinite; at least 8s before pre-signal | Function, ownership, destination, whether anything will happen |
| Pre-signal | One faint point advances intermittently across part of the dark | Is that movement, reflection, or signal? | Silence | 7–10s | Source, endpoint, purpose, whether it is meaningful |
| Relation | The point completes a thin relation between the unknown source and one station node | Is the station receiving it? | `carrier present` | 12–16s | Whether the node received, rejected, or merely intersected it |
| Optional attention | If attended, the local trace stops flickering and becomes easier to follow; otherwise it continues unchanged | Can this be perceived more clearly? | `locally stable` | Held while attended; otherwise at least 10s | Content, identity, intention, intended listener |
| Aftereffect | The station node changes halo pattern and colour after the carrier reaches it | Did the node change because of the carrier? | `receiver state changed` | 16–22s | What changed internally, whether anything was returned, whether this is routine |
| Quiet | The trace thins, the node settles into a quiet available state, unrelated traffic continues | Will this relation appear again? | Silence | Indefinite | Source, meaning, future recurrence, complete outcome |

## Variant B — legible

The same event is easier to follow. The rail confirms causal conditions only
after the canvas has already produced the question.

| Moment | Canvas change | Possible question forming | Rail contribution | Minimum pause | Remains unresolved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quiet | Stable exterior with one visibly available station node | What does the structure receive? | `local reception available` | Indefinite; at least 8s before pre-signal | What may arrive and whether availability is invitation |
| Pre-signal | A pulse appears in three separated positions, establishing direction without a full line | Where is it going? | Silence | 6–9s | Source class, destination, content, urgency |
| Relation | The pulse reaches the station node; only then does a faint connecting trace resolve | Has something reached local reception? | `carrier entered local reception` | 12–16s | Whether reception is complete and who, if anyone, requested it |
| Optional attention | Attention holds the trace at a consistent intensity; the world continues behind it | Does listening reveal more without sending anything? | `reply not requested` | Held while attended; otherwise at least 10s | Carrier wording, motive, sender, intended audience |
| Aftereffect | The receiving node changes state independently of attention; no wider station activation follows | Was the carrier received? | `node condition changed after arrival` | 16–22s | Whether this is acknowledgement, routing, storage, or routine processing |
| Quiet | Relation fades; the receiving node remains available; Observatory approach is still possible | Is the encounter over, or merely quiet again? | `action required: none` | Indefinite | Later relation, source identity, what the station retained |

## Variant C — mysterious

The rail remains nearly absent. Spatial relation, timing, and recurrence of form
carry the encounter without declaring what kind of process occurred.

| Moment | Canvas change | Possible question forming | Rail contribution | Minimum pause | Remains unresolved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quiet | The exterior remains whole and unlabelled beyond the location mark | Am I looking at a station, vessel, habitat, or something else? | Silence | Indefinite; at least 10s before pre-signal | Almost everything beyond presence and scale |
| Pre-signal | A dim point appears, vanishes, then appears closer to the station | Did the same thing move? | Silence | 9–12s | Continuity, direction, source, whether the points are related |
| Relation | For one interval, source and node share the same faint cadence; no permanent line appears | Are those two objects responding to the same process? | `pattern coincident` | 14–18s | Carrier versus coincidence, sender versus receiver, causality |
| Optional attention | Attention slows the local cadence enough to compare both endpoints; it reveals no words | Is the pattern shared or only locally reconstructed? | Silence | Held while attended; otherwise at least 12s | Process, message, agency, whether attention improved truth or only visibility |
| Aftereffect | The station node keeps the cadence for one additional cycle after the source trace disappears | Did something remain at the node? | `source unresolved` | 18–24s | Receipt, memory, response, routine, relation to the vanished source |
| Quiet | The node and field return to their earlier rhythms; other traffic remains | Did anything finish? | Silence | Indefinite | Whether the event completed, will recur, or was interpreted correctly |

## Smallest version worth implementing

Implement **Variant A — quiet**, with only two rail facts:

```text
relation     carrier present
aftereffect  receiver state changed
```

Optional attention stabilizes the local trace but reveals no words. Do not add
a return pulse or same-session recurrence yet. Let the carrier reach the node,
let the node visibly change afterward, and let both settle back into an
indefinite inhabited quiet.

This is the smallest cut that can test all essential claims:

```text
canvas creates the question before language
world event exists without participation
attention changes resolution only
receiving has a visible consequence
silence remains playable
return to quiet remains a living state
```

## Falsification

The experiment is falsified if a fresh viewer's primary account is:

> “I clicked/listened, and that made the station respond to me.”

It is also falsified if viewers consistently report that nothing happened until
they acted, that the carrier was obviously addressed to them, that the final
quiet felt like failure/loading, or that no question formed before the rail
named the event.

If those responses appear, adding more copy is not the remedy. The canvas
timing, causal sequence, and independence of the aftereffect must be revised.
