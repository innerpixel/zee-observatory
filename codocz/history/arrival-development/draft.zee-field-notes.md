# Zee's Field Notes — first drafts

*Fragments from the notebook. Not chapters. Non-sequential on purpose — the log
should feel older than what we've seen of it.*

*Form under test: **expectation → observation → revision.** Zee is visibly
changed by contact, which is the collection's closing line performed by the
narrator instead of stated by it.*

---

### ENTRY 003 · On the phrase "Thank you for sharing"

**Expected:** an expression of gratitude. Information received, sender thanked.

**Observed:** the phrase occurs most often immediately before a conversation
returns to its previous position. In eleven of fourteen recorded instances,
nothing that followed it differed from what would have followed silence.

**Revised:** it is not gratitude. It is punctuation. The humans have built a
sound meaning *this portion is now complete*, and they have made it warm so it
does not feel like a door closing.

I do not think they know they are doing this. Mara says she does it too.

---

### ENTRY 007 · The physics of almost

**Expected:** a raised hand and an almost-raised hand should be nearly the same
event. The difference is a few centimetres.

**Observed:** they are not the same event. They are not the same category. An
almost-raised hand produces no sound, occupies no time, and enters no record.
It is the loudest thing in the room to exactly one person.

**Revised:** on Earth, *almost* is quieter than *nothing*. Nothing at least
leaves the space empty. Almost fills it and then apologises.

---

### ENTRY 011 · The dentist

Mara explained a procedure to me. You arrive and say: I have a discomfort here.
The specialist is trained for exactly this. They look. They investigate a small
signal before it becomes a large one. This is the entire purpose of the
building.

She then explained that sometimes the specialist does not look. Sometimes they
recommend a different and more expensive procedure, and the discomfort goes home
still carrying itself.

I asked whether the building had changed purpose.

She said no. She said the building keeps its purpose in writing.

I have added a category to the notebook: *institutions that retain their stated
function while quietly declining to perform it.* The category is large. I have
had to make the page bigger twice.

---

### ENTRY 014 · On nodding

**Expected:** a nod indicates agreement, or at minimum, reception.

**Observed:** eleven nods occurred. The plan did not move. Therefore the nods
were not reception — reception leaves marks.

**Revised:** a nod appears to mean *I have registered that you are speaking and
I do not wish you harm.* This is a real message and I have been unfair to it.
It is simply not the message the speaker was listening for.

Two true things arrived in the same moment and only one of them was heard. I am
told this also happens on my world. We merely have the advantage of being able
to see it.

### ENTRY 019 · A design defect

On my world, feelings do not stay inside. They gather in the room and everyone
stands in the same weather.

I described this to Mara as an advantage. She asked what happens when a person
on my world is ashamed.

I said: everyone knows.

She asked what happens when a person on my world wishes to be sad without an
audience.

I said: this is not possible.

She said: we call that privacy. We built rooms for it.

I have recorded Earth's inability to share weather as a limitation for one
hundred and six days. I am revising the entry. It may be a limitation. It is
also a wall a person can stand behind, and I have never had one.

---

### ENTRY 022 · The Vessel-folk *(report, unverified)*

I have not visited them. This is Mara's favourite class of source, which she
calls a rumour and I call insufficient data.

It is said that among the Vessel-folk any expressed discomfort must be
investigated before the day closes. No signal may be carried overnight. It is
their oldest law and they are proud of it.

It is also said that among the Vessel-folk no one may say *it is nothing.* Not
because they are forbidden. Because the sentence has no listeners.

A person there who wishes to hold something quietly, privately, until they
understand it themselves, has nowhere to put it. Their discomfort belongs to the
room the moment it becomes visible.

I had begun to think of them as the solution.

I am now writing a second column.

---

### ENTRY 026

Humans say *I hear you.*

Both of us know this is not the measurement.

The measurement is what moves afterward.

## What emerged

**The form works, but not for everything.** 003, 007, 014 are clean
expectation → observation → revision. 011, 019, 022 refused it and became small
scenes instead — and they're the better ones. Proposed rule: the tri-part form
is for phenomena, plain narration is for encounters with Mara. Don't force it.

**Mara is the revision mechanism.** In four of seven notes she is the reason
Zee's understanding moves. She isn't a companion in these — she's the friction
that makes revision happen. Worth keeping deliberately.

**Nobody is wrong in any of them.** 003 rescues the phrase-users ("Mara says she
does it too"), 014 rescues the nodders. Held to the tone constraint without
effort, which suggests the constraint is well-matched to the material.

**Zee's world has a cost now** (019) and the first other species has one too
(022). The Vessel-folk solve the dentist problem completely and create a new
one: nowhere to put something you're not ready to share. Keeps the homeworld
from becoming instruction.
