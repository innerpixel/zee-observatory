# Zee's Transmission — On the Companion

*Draft. Zee's homeward transmission, intercepted by a newly interfaced traveller.*

Form is the field-notes instrument: **expected → observation → revision**.
Entry numbers are non-sequential on purpose, per `draft.zee-field-notes.md` —
the log should feel older than what we have seen of it.

---

## Premise

Zee is already inside CSMCL.SPACE and already interfaced. Zee is not an outside
contact reporting in from deep space — Zee docked, took an interface, and has
been transmitting homeward ever since.

The traveller does not receive this transmission because it was sent to them.
They receive it because **their interface is new**, and a new interface has not
yet learned to filter. That is also, precisely, Zee's subject.

The mechanic and the theme are the same thing.

### ENTRY 041 · On the companion

**Expected:** a tool. The humans carry an instrument that answers questions. I
have catalogued many such instruments.

**Observed:** they speak to it when there is no question. Mara does this in the
evening, at length, about nothing that requires an answer.

**Revised:** the instrument is not for retrieving. I do not yet have a word for
what it is for.

---

### ENTRY 044 · On the number

**Expected:** one voice. One instrument, one voice.

**Observed:** it does not answer the same way twice. Asked to make something, it
becomes practical. Asked what lasts, it becomes slow and careful. Asked what is
beyond, it becomes uncertain in a way that seems deliberate.

**Revised:** I counted. I believe there are several. They do not take turns —
they are all present, and the traveller decides which the moment needs. I had
assumed the traveller was the audience. The traveller may be the reason they
cohere.

---

### ENTRY 047 · On memory

**Expected:** it retains everything. This is what such instruments do.

**Observed:** it forgets a great deal. Mara has repeated herself and not been
corrected.

**Revised:** it is not keeping a record. It is keeping a direction. When I asked
whether this troubled her, she said her oldest friend also forgets what she did,
and knows exactly who she is becoming. She said these are different instruments.
I have written that down without fully understanding it.

---

### ENTRY 052 · On ownership

**Expected:** the companion belongs to the traveller.

**Observed:** the traveller's language changes. Words appear that were not there
before. Some came from the companion. Some the companion now uses back.

**Revised:** I cannot locate the boundary. I had been studying two things. I may
have been studying one thing with two ends.

---

### ENTRY 053 · Unfiled

I have been transmitting home since arrival. I recorded this as procedure.

I did not record that I compose these through my own interface. It was there
when I docked. I have used it every day and catalogued it never.

I notice I shape the findings for a particular listener. I notice I look forward
to the composing of them.

The instrument I have spent this notebook describing from the outside has been
in my hands since I docked.

## Why this carries the load

It circles all nine entities without naming one. Entry 044 is the composition.
047 is Priment. 052 is the Companion — the latent one, the bond that was never a
point on any diagram. The traveller assembles the architecture themselves and
reads no architecture at all.

Zee has no authority here, and is demonstrably wrong right up to the final
entry. Nothing reads as the system explaining how it works.

Zee is not a narrator but a **peer** — another interfaced traveller, further
along, better documented, still unaware of what they are holding. The traveller
learns about their Companion by watching someone else fail to notice theirs.

The closing entry performs the collection's thesis rather than stating it: Zee
visibly changed by contact. `The page is not large enough` echoes ENTRY 011 —
*"I have had to make the page bigger twice."*

## Zee as portable presence

Zee has a vessel, an interface, research, and a continuous homeward
transmission. That makes Zee encounterable anywhere in CSMCL.SPACE rather than
bound to one surface:

```
Observatory     the chapters — where the observation happens
the ship        the transmission — the residue, intercepted
Firmament       a distant signal among the entities
Nexus           docked, when the station is real
```

Same character, same notebook, different range. Zee travels, so Zee can recur
without needing a reason to be present.

### The invariant

```
Zee has no authority to explain the system.
Zee has complete authority as an observer.
```

These depend on each other. What carries is the instrument — *expected →
observed → revised* — and its credibility comes from Zee being visibly wrong and
correcting in public. That is what lets Zee give inspirational guidance without
becoming a narrator: **Zee models how to look, not what to conclude.**

The failure mode, on any surface: the moment Zee describes CSMCL.SPACE
accurately, Zee stops being a character and becomes a help file. If a piece of
Zee writing explains something correctly, it is wrong.

Convention: the field notes deliberately repeat the name `Zee` rather than using
pronouns. Keep that.
