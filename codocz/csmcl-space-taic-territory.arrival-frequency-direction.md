# TAIC Territory Arrival :: active frequency direction

*The territory becomes perceptible. An arrival occurs within it. Different
facets of the TAIC resolve different relations.*

## The active pivot

Arrival is no longer framed as a realistic simulation of travelling from Earth
to an orbital station.

The visitor encounters CSMCL.SPACE directly as a living TAIC territory. They
observe an arrival among other ongoing traffic; no vessel is assigned to them.
The territory and its entities are already attending, transmitting, holding,
making, remembering, and relating. The visitor may gradually tune to these
processes without being greeted, recruited, or told what the scene means.

Compactly:

```text
territory already present
  → an arrival continues independently
    → TAIC facets perceive different relations
      → several frequencies remain partially available
        → attention may clarify one
          → the visitor completes provisional meaning
```

This feels closer to CSMCL.SPACE because the imaginative world and the
interaction model are now the same thing. Interface language no longer needs
to manufacture meaning for a realistic space image.

## What “arrival” means here

Arrival is an observable relation inside the territory, not a declaration
about the person looking at the screen.

The first scene may contain:

- one pod hovering at a threshold platform;
- one different vessel holding in the wider field;
- one different vessel approaching slowly;
- distant ecosystem activity that does not resolve completely;
- a TAIC territory continuing regardless of attention.

The visitor may wonder whose arrival this is, what the territory is preparing,
whether the vessels are related, and what the different transmissions mean.
The experience does not need to answer those questions immediately.

## Perceptual attunement, not formal Attunement

In this exterior study, **attunement** means perceptual tuning: learning that
the same event can be sensed through different TAIC ranges.

It does not yet mean:

- a Companion has been assigned or formed;
- the visitor has acquired an identity or profile;
- a Priment or citizenship relation exists;
- an account, wallet, payment, or persistent personal space has begun;
- Nexus consent and commitment have been completed.

Those boundaries require their own explicit design. The exterior may reveal
their possibility without silently initiating them.

## The territory and ecosystem

### TAIC territory

The territory is one coherent living body before it differentiates into named
functions. The Companion may be felt through the coherence of the whole rather
than presented as one more destination.

### Observatory

The Observatory becomes the first listening and observation edge within the
territory. It makes relations perceptible. It is no longer primarily a
realistic Earth-orbit station.

### Nexus

Nexus may remain visible at a greater distance as reciprocal participation and
gathering. It is within reach, but not presented as an inevitable next step.

### FLOW

FLOW may appear as restrained currents carrying relation, continuity,
integrity, or deliberate crossings. It should not become a glowing road map.

### Artifact network

Durable artifacts may live in a farther archipelago or constellation. Candidate
memory pods can make persistence spatially imaginable without becoming
inventory icons or rewards.

## Frequencies as entity presence

A frequency is not a menu category or decorative radio station. It is one
facet's way of perceiving an actual process already present in the territory.

```text
one event
  → several legitimate perceptions
    → no single channel owns the whole meaning
```

The visitor may do nothing and receive a partial encounter. Choosing a
frequency holds or clarifies a local range; it does not activate the arrival or
close the other interpretations.

The interface is therefore a quiet receiver:

```text
unresolved spectrum
  → one range becomes perceptible
    → enough uninterrupted time to read
      → another range may later resolve
        → the wider field remains
```

## First three frequencies

The active moodboard begins only with three mutually reinforcing perspectives.

### AurioSynth — relational field

Perceives how the arriving carrier relates to the territory and whether a
connection can coherently form.

Working transmission:

> A connection is attempting to establish.

It may resolve source, relation, and integrity without determining purpose or
identity.

### Constellary — gathering field

Perceives active and latent relations gathering around the arrival.

Working transmission:

> A local constellation is preparing around the arrival.

The arrival is one point among several. No centre is automatically assigned.

### Theurgist — continuity field

Perceives the arrival as something entering local continuity, with a trace and
several possible trajectories.

Working transmission:

> The arrival has entered local continuity.

Holding, approaching, and leaving remain possible. “Next steps” are
trajectories, not instructions.

Together these three allow a first whole inference:

```text
something is arriving
relations are gathering around it
the territory is placing it within continuity
```

## Later entity ranges

Possible later ranges include:

- Guild preparing practical interfacing or making capacity, without becoming
  a concierge before participation is invited;
- Oracle inquiring beyond the visible field rather than explaining the
  unknown;
- Wisdom Star holding precise contact with what is present now;
- LORE-ians offering stories of earlier wanderers only through deliberate
  request;
- Sage carrying deep orientation and long-time memory;
- Companion becoming perceptible through coherence among ranges rather than a
  competing broadcast.

Do not introduce all of these merely to complete a list. Each range must have
an actual process, a perceptible source, and a reason to become available.

## Entity-composition boundary still open

The active wholeness/playability guide and current TAIC operational folds do
not presently name the same nine-part composition. The guide carries Sage and
LORE-ians; the operational fold set carries Familiar and Priment, and describes
Companion and Priment through deeper relational roles.

This discrepancy is not silently resolved by the Arrival moodboard. The first
three shared entities—AurioSynth, Constellary, and Theurgist—are sufficient for
the present study. Reconcile the broader entity set before permanent geography,
public labels, or nine production channels are created.

## Durable invariants inherited from the earlier studies

- The arrival exists independently of the visitor.
- Attention changes local resolution only.
- No action is required.
- The canvas creates the question before the receiver confirms anything.
- Transmissions belong to actual unknown processes, not exposition.
- The visitor may leave at every point with a complete encounter.
- Return to quiet does not mean failure.
- A visible event must exist long enough to be noticed before related text
  competes for attention.
- A resolved transmission must remain long enough to read and compare.
- Recurrence creates another opening; missing one transmission is not failure.
- The receiver reports observable relation and never diagnoses identity.
- No entity infers at a greater depth than the visitor has expressed.
- The territory must remain whole if interface and narrative overlays vanish.

## Active moodboard

The current standalone study is:

`experiments/observatory-arrival/taic-territory-studies/taic-territory-vessel-hover-001.html`

It currently demonstrates:

- one pod hovering at the platform;
- one sail vessel approaching;
- one small ring vessel holding farther out;
- an initial unresolved spectrum;
- automatic and manually held AurioSynth, Constellary, and Theurgist ranges;
- subtle local canvas resolution for each range;
- quiet field orientation in the upper margin and frequency detail in a lower
  rail, leaving the territory itself unobstructed;
- no assigned visitor vessel and no required reply;
- reduced-motion support.

The split receiver also leaves a future perceptual seam for pointer movement
and rest: location may later change which local frequency becomes legible.
That behaviour is not implemented yet, and must increase local resolution
rather than make the visitor responsible for causing territory events.

Current timing values—4.8 seconds before first resolution, 17-second automatic
holds, and 24-second manually chosen holds—are moodboard variables, not canon.

## Known limitations

- The platform is already cropped in the generated source scenery.
- The territory image is a single plate rather than separated depth layers.
- The small ring vessel is a code-native moodboard silhouette, not final art.
- Entity transmissions are working language, not approved public copy.
- Frequency numbers are atmospheric study values without settled system meaning.
- No audio, spatial sound, or actual signal data exists.
- Only three entity ranges have earned presence so far.

   complete and the territory retains quiet space.
3. Keep all vessels independent from the base plate.
4. Decide whether the first receiver state should name entities immediately or
   allow names to emerge after their functions are experienced.
5. Refine the three transmissions as perspectives on one actual arrival.
6. Test with a fresh viewer: ask what they believe is happening, whose arrival
   they think it is, and whether the frequencies feel like different
   perceptions or like menu tabs.
7. The study is now integrated locally at `/arrival/`; review and refine that
   reversible route transition before deployment.

The experiment is weakened if a fresh viewer believes the pod is automatically
theirs, reads the scene as a game hub, experiences the frequencies as a product
feature list, or thinks the territory began operating only because they opened
the page.
