# Zee · Reference Traveller 001

## Purpose

Zee is the Observatory's deliberate reference traveller: one continuous
presence through which arrivals, public accounts, transmissions, resonance,
privacy, artifacts, and ecosystem relations can become concrete.

Zee is a case study, not the template for a traveller. Another traveller's
CSMCL.Space may have a completely different character, visual language,
organization, and degree of public presence.

## Structural position

```text
Zee's private CSMCL.Space
        |
        | deliberate publication
        v
Zee's independent public projection
        |
        | observed and contextualized by
        v
CSMCL.SPACE Observatory · Case Study 001
```

The Observatory does not own Zee, operate Zee's inner space, or imply access
to everything Zee holds. It can present only the traces Zee has deliberately
made observable.

This case study keeps the present work inside the Observatory scope. It does
not establish traveller accounts, authentication, profile management, or a
general publication system.

## Stable reference facts

- Public name: Zee.
- Reference designation: Reference Traveller 001.
- Self-description currently available: field observer · unfinished listener.
- Zee comes from beyond the blue planet and maintains contact with a home
  world.
- Zee is curious about how unfamiliar beings receive one another's realities.
- Zee may appear throughout the ecosystem when a concrete traveller is needed
  to explore an account, arrival, interaction, transmission, artifact, or
  system relation.

Stable facts should remain coherent wherever Zee appears. New facts are not
stable merely because they were useful in one design scenario.

## Public projection · initial

The first Observatory case study may expose only:

- Zee's chosen name and short public orientation;
- selected interests that may resonate with other travellers;
- one observable arrival trace;
- the existence of communication with Zee's home world;
- selected public fragments or artifacts when Zee chooses to share them;
- a visible statement that other material remains private.

Public absence is valid. Empty categories do not need placeholder content.

## Private material

The following material is not part of the public case study:

- the interactive human-interaction field study;
- instruments and working methods;
- unfinished findings and encounter analysis;
- private field notes and local-device notes;
- internal chapters, choices, and developmental transmissions;
- any material not deliberately crossed into the public projection.

The retained source currently lives at:

`experiments/zee-reference-traveller/private-field-study/`

Placing private material behind an unlinked public URL would not make it
private. Until access control exists, private studies remain outside the
public application source and build.

## Scenario layer

Zee may be used to study future possibilities such as:

- first arrival and return;
- public account representation;
- private-to-public publication;
- resonance interests and discovery;
- traveller-to-traveller contact;
- TAIC interactions and transmissions;
- artifacts moving between personal and collective space;
- communication between CSMCL.SPACE and Zee's home world.

Scenario material is provisional unless explicitly promoted into Zee's stable
reference facts or public projection.

## Public presentation invariants

- Say "case study" or "reference traveller" when Observatory context matters.
- Do not describe Zee as a universal traveller model.
- Do not expose private study mechanics to demonstrate product capability.
- Do not imply that observing Zee grants access to Zee's inner space.
- Do not turn interests into profile metrics or engagement scores.
- Let selected traces suggest possibility without prescribing what another
  traveller's CSMCL.Space should become.
